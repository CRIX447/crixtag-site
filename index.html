Four ways to play

Pick a mode when you make a room. Everyone joins with the same code.

Casual

Plain tag. One player is it, they pass it on by touching someone, and it never really ends.

Infection

The infected side grows every time they catch someone. Last one not infected wins the round.

Freeze tag

Get tagged and you're stuck where you stand until a teammate reaches you and unfreezes you.

Modded

Rooms where mod menus are allowed. Fling people around, break the physics, see what happens.

Spawn is where everyone hangs out

It's a place to stand around in, not just a menu you walk through.

  • A computer for joining rooms and changing your settings
  • A wardrobe for swapping your cosmetics
  • Music, and a chill corner to sit in
  • A TV playing Crix's videos
  • A leaderboard for the top players
  • A statue that lights up whenever Crix is online

A studio built for filming

If you make videos, you shouldn't have to fight the game to get a decent shot. The recording studio is in at launch.

  • Mixed reality camera support
  • A green screen you can film against
  • Swap the backdrop colour to whatever you're editing on
  • Camera stands, so you can put the shot where you want it and leave it

Creator badges

Post videos about Crix Tag and you can earn a badge that shows up in game. Three tiers, each one harder than the last.

  1. Creator

    Five decent videos about Crix Tag on YouTube or TikTok, 50 subscribers or followers, and 2,500 total views.

  2. Verified creator

    Ten strong videos, 500 subscribers or followers, and 10,000 total views.

  3. Paw painter

    Over 50 videos, 1,500 subscribers or followers, and 100,000 total views.

Hats, shirts and Banana Bucks

Banana Bucks are the in-game currency. Spend them in the Shop map on anything in the wardrobe.

Cosmetics

Top hats, party hats, banana hats, coconut hats, caps and headphones. Shirts in black, white and one that says I heart Crix Tag. Sunglasses and normal glasses.

VIP

A golden top hat, a golden nametag, a VIP badge and shirt, access to the VIP room, a Discord role, plus the fling hammer and money gun.

Mod menus

Tools for modded rooms only, so normal games stay normal. Bundles are also planned for people who want a bit of everything.
