How you play

Pick a mode when you make a room. Everyone joins with the same code.

Casual

Plain tag. One player is it, they pass it on by touching someone, and it never really ends.

Infection

The infected side grows every time they catch someone. Last one not infected wins the round.

Modded

Rooms where mod menus are allowed. Fling people around, break the physics, see what happens.

Freeze tag Later

Get tagged and you're stuck where you stand until a teammate reaches you. Coming in an update after launch.

Take a look around

Six maps are being built for launch. Flick through them.

Spawn

The hub everyone lands in. There's a wardrobe for changing your cosmetics, a leaderboard for the top players, music, a corner to sit in, a TV playing Crix's videos, and a statue that lights up whenever Crix is online.

Forest

The main tag map, and where most rounds will happen. Trees to climb, branches to swing off, and long gaps that punish you for missing.

Shop

Where Banana Bucks turn into hats. The stock changes over time, so it's worth checking back. You can see what's in there right now on the store page.

Movie theatre

A room with a big screen playing Crix's videos, and livestreams while they're running. Grab a seat and watch with whoever else is in there.

Fanart room

A gallery hung with art sent in by players. Draw something and post it in the Discord to get it on the wall.

Recording studio

A room built for filming, with a green screen, camera stands and backdrop colours you can change. More on it further down the page.

A studio built for filming

If you make videos, you shouldn't have to fight the game to get a decent shot. The recording studio is in at launch.

  • Mixed reality camera support
  • A green screen you can film against
  • Swap the backdrop colour to whatever you're editing on
  • Camera stands, so you can set the shot and leave it

Creator badges

Post videos about Crix Tag and you can earn a badge that shows up next to your name in game. Three levels, each one harder than the last.

  1. Creator

    Five decent videos about Crix Tag on YouTube or TikTok · 50 subscribers or followers · 2,500 total views

  2. Verified creator

    Ten high quality videos on YouTube or TikTok · 500 subscribers or followers · 10,000 total views

  3. Paw painter

    Over 50 videos on YouTube or TikTok · 1,500 subscribers or followers · 100,000 total views

Once you hit a level, post in the Discord to claim it.

Hats, shirts and Banana Bucks
