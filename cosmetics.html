Special cosmetics

Some of the rarer things you'll be able to wear and carry. Everything here is still being made, so the art can change before it lands in game.

VIP

A one-time payment. Buy it once and it stays on your account — there's no subscription and nothing to renew.

VIP also gets you into the VIP room and an exclusive role in the Discord.

Creator badges

Earned, not bought. Make videos about Crix Tag on YouTube or TikTok and the badge shows up next to your name in game.

  1. Creator

    • 5 decent quality videos about Crix Tag
    • 50 subscribers or followers
    • 2,500 total views

  2. Verified creator

    • 10 high quality videos about Crix Tag
    • 500 subscribers or followers
    • 10,000 total views

  3. Paw painter

    • Over 50 videos about Crix Tag
    • 1,500 subscribers or followers
    • 100,000 total views

Videos on either platform count towards the same badge. Once you've hit a level, post in the Discord to claim it.

Staff tools

These aren't for sale and can't be earned. They go to moderators, developers and Crix, and they exist so someone can step in when a room goes bad.

Moderator stick

The everyday tool moderators carry for keeping a room in order.

Fling hammer

The same hammer VIPs get, but with the cooldown removed.

Kick hammer

Removes someone from the room. Given only to trusted moderators and above.

Ban hammer

Stops someone getting back into the game at all. Owners only.

Admin menu

Spawns items, hands out Banana Bucks and gives out cosmetics. Works in any room, not just modded ones. Developers and Crix only.

Discord button

A shortcut that opens the Discord invite. Moderators and above.

Cookie top hat

One of a kind. Only Crix wears this one.
